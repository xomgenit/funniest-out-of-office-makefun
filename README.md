Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Funniest Out-of-Office"](https://make.fun/c/u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf): one line of an out-of-office auto-reply, the funniest I could make it, in 140 characters or fewer.

> I am out until Monday. I asked my plants to water my inbox. They declined, politely, in writing.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `out`).
